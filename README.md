
# DREAM DECOR
### Interior Design • Architecture • Construction

Dream Decor is a design and construction brand based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, focused on creating thoughtfully designed spaces through quality workmanship, creative design, and practical construction solutions.

## About Us

Dream Decor provides interior design, architectural planning, 3D visualization, and civil construction services for residential and commercial projects. Our goal is to bring clients' ideas to life through functional layouts, elegant aesthetics, and reliable project execution.
